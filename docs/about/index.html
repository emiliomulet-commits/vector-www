Who we are

Vector Consulting Limited is a technology consultancy registered in Hong Kong. Our work is concentrated in regulated, data-intensive sectors where security, compliance and reliability are not optional.

Our mission is to give ambitious companies access to senior technology expertise with the flexibility, transparency and accountability of a long-term partner.

We work with senior technicians in several countries, including specialists in London through Technova Corp Ltd, to give clients the right expertise wherever it is.
