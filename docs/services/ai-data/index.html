AI & Data

Applied AI and trusted data, built for European standards

From first use case to production, we help you adopt AI and data responsibly and at scale.

Capabilities

Applied AI

Document understanding, assistants and decision support built on your real data, evaluated against a measurable baseline.

Sovereign AI

Open-weight models running in your cloud tenant or on-premise, so sensitive data never leaves your control.

Data platforms

Pipelines, warehouses and APIs that turn scattered data into a reliable, documented asset.

Federated data spaces

Connectors, usage policies (ODRL), identity and trust lists on Gaia-X, IDSA and Pontus-X principles.

Governance & compliance

GDPR, EU AI Act and EHDS requirements translated into architecture, logging and human oversight.

MLOps

Monitoring, evaluation and versioning so models keep performing after go-live.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Start from a decision

    We pick one concrete business decision and the data it needs, which keeps scope and privacy review small.

  2. 02

    Bring the model to the data

    Where data cannot travel, we deploy the model next to it.

  3. 03

    Design governance in

    Logging, evaluation and human oversight are part of the architecture.

  4. 04

    Measure, then scale

    Every pilot has a baseline, a target and an owner before it grows.
