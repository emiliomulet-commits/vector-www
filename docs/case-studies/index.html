Global Data Care: a federated health data space

Challenge

Create a trusted way for hospitals, research groups and health technology companies to share data without giving up control of it.

What we did

Vector delivered the full technology development and the technological and functional consulting: federated architecture, connectors, usage policies, identity and trust lists, and the participant onboarding flow.

Outcome

A working federated health data space in which each participant keeps its data at source and decides, policy by policy, who can use it and for what.
