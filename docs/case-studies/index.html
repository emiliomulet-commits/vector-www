Global Data Care: a federated health data space

Transcript

How do hospitals share data without giving it away? Hospitals, researchers and health-tech companies each keep their data at source. The data stays home. What travels is a usage policy: who, for what, and until when. Only verified participants on the trust list can join. Patient consent is enforced on the server, not just hidden on screen. A working federated health data space, built on European standards. Global Data Care. Built by Vector for Accuro Technology.

Challenge

Create a trusted way for hospitals, research groups and health technology companies to share data without giving up control of it.

What we did

Vector delivered the full technology development and the technological and functional consulting: federated architecture, connectors, usage policies, identity and trust lists, and the participant onboarding flow.

Outcome

A working federated health data space in which each participant keeps its data at source and decides, policy by policy, who can use it and for what.

PROCUREDATA: a procurement data space

Transcript

How many times does a supplier type the same data? The same certificate, re-entered in portal after portal. With PROCUREDATA, the supplier publishes it once, as a data package. Nothing flows without an approved transaction and a valid usage policy. The buyer's ERP fills in the form automatically, on approval or on demand. AI proposes; the policy decides. And there is no central data store. Data entered once, consumed under governance. PROCUREDATA.

Challenge

Help buyers and suppliers share procurement data in a reliable, interoperable way.

What we did

Vector advises on the federated architecture and the data space design, bringing the patterns proven in the health sector.

Outcome

A federated procurement platform design aligned with European data space standards.
