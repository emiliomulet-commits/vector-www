Privacy policy

How Vector Consulting Limited collects and uses personal data through its website and email.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Vector Consulting Limited, Business Registration No. 72876645, Rooms 1705-1709, 17/F, Tung Chiu Commercial Centre, 193 Lockhart Road, Wanchai, Hong Kong. For any question about your data, write to alison.lindsay@vectorconsultingltd.com.

What data we collect

We only collect the data you choose to send us: through the contact form (name, company, email address and your message) or by writing to us by email. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

We use your data to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a proposal. The legal basis is the steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). For residents of Hong Kong, we process your data in line with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486). We do not use your data for automated decisions or send you marketing without your consent.

Who processes it for us

To run the website and handle messages we rely on these providers, who act on our behalf: GitHub (website hosting, which records technical access logs such as IP addresses), FormSubmit (delivery of the contact form to our mailbox) and Microsoft 365 (email). Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases transfers rely on the safeguards offered by each provider, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, or are necessary to answer the enquiry you send us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to answer it and follow up, and for a maximum of two years if no business relationship results, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format, by writing to alison.lindsay@vectorconsultingltd.com. If you are in the European Union you may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es). In Hong Kong, the competent authority is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (www.pcpd.org.hk).

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and does not store your data in any database. Messages are received only in our corporate mailbox.
